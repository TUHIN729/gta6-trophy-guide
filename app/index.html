    The road to the Platinum

    About 200 hours, rated 4/10 by PowerPyx, in a single playthrough — there is no mission replay and no New Game+, but free roam stays open after the story. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter: 15 save slots, and no way back otherwise.

    1. Phase 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

      Play all five chapters with Jason and Lucia and take optional missions as they appear. Story trophies pop as you go and cannot be missed. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars from the start — the 100% checklist will want 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of 255 animals later.

      Do not skip the balcony meditation. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditating at Jason’s house in the morning. Follow her to the balcony and accept — that spot is gone after Chapter 1, and Positive Mental Attitude may need it.

    2. Phase 2 — 100% completion about 100 hours

      After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist: 100 optional tasks, all 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, all 8 bank vaults, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 of 125 Cocaine Flamingos, 200 vehicles, 50 weapons, 100 animals, all 42 shooting-range golds, all 42 drone time trials, 300 of 397 stunts and 70 of 80 challenges. The checklist is roughly 60% of true completion — do not chase everything.

    3. Phase 3 — Strangers, side strands & cleanup 10–20 hours

      Finish the eight qualifying Strangers & Freaks strands, the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, the remaining meditation spots, and any combat, driving or robbery trophies still open. Then check the list here: when the last one ticks, the Platinum pops.

    Know before you start

    • Cheats disable trophies and saving while active — keep trophy runs cheat-free and save before you cheat.
    • Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Everything else, story included, can be earned after the credits.
    • No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no DLC trophies. The whole list lives in Leonida.

    About this list

    Grand Theft Auto VI has 55 trophies on PlayStation 5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. The same 55 exist as achievements on Xbox Series X|S, worth 1,000 Gamerscore. All 55 are single-player and offline.

    Rockstar has not published an official trophy list. The names, descriptions and grades in this guide are the ones reported on 18 November 2026 by the guide sites below, working from the game, and they agree with each other. Where those guides have not yet confirmed how a trophy unlocks, this guide says so in the trophy’s own entry instead of guessing — five trophies are unconfirmed that way, and one more has an unconfirmed detail.
